    <h2>About</h2>
    <div class="row">
      <div>
      <p> The BOS Webinar Series is a new initiative
         launched by the  <a href="https://bileveloptimization.org/">Bilevel Optimization Society (BOS)</a>,
         a technical section of the <a href="http://www.mathopt.org/">Mathematical Optimization Society (MOS)</a>. This online seminar series is open to anyone interested in bilevel optimization, including researchers, students, and practitioners, and aims to strengthen connections within the bilevel optimization community by offering a bi-weekly platform to present new ideas, share recent results, and discuss open questions. Topics of interest include theoretical, computational, and applied aspects of bilevel optimization.
      </p>

      <p>The webinar series features two possible formats:
        <ul>
          <li>one 40-minute talk, or</li>
          <li>two 20-minute talks,</li>
        </ul>
        each followed by discussion.</p>

        <p>Please become a BOS member to receive updates and Microsoft Teams links for upcoming BOS webinars. To become a BOS member, we invite you to fill the
