About

The BOS Webinar Series is a new initiative launched by the Bilevel Optimization Society (BOS), a technical section of the Mathematical Optimization Society (MOS). This online seminar series is open to anyone interested in bilevel optimization, including researchers, students, and practitioners, and aims to strengthen connections within the bilevel optimization community by offering a bi-weekly platform to present new ideas, share recent results, and discuss open questions. Topics of interest include theoretical, computational, and applied aspects of bilevel optimization.

The webinar series features two possible formats:

  • one 40-minute talk, or
  • two 20-minute talks,
each followed by discussion.

Organization

Seminars are scheduled bi-weekly on Tuesdays at 4:00 pm CET (Central European Time) and are held on Zoom.
