##### Call for hosts for ICBO 2028

Following [ICBO 2026 in Pittsburgh](/icbo/2026/index.html), the Conference Site Advisory Committee invites expressions of interest in hosting the **2028 International Conference on Bilevel Optimization (ICBO)**.

**To express interest, email [icbo@bileveloptimization.org](mailto:icbo@bileveloptimization.org) by December 20, 2026.** A brief note is enough. Please include the proposed location and host institution(s), the members of your organizing team, and tentative conference dates.

Full proposals of one to two pages are due by **January 30, 2027** and should describe the venue, logistical arrangements, and preliminary budget. The committee will provide further guidance after the expression-of-interest stage and aims to select the host by the end of February 2027.

For questions about the call, contact Oleg Prokopyev at [oleg.prokopyev@business.uzh.ch](mailto:oleg.prokopyev@business.uzh.ch). For practical questions about organizing the conference, contact ICBO 2026 co-organizer Bo Zeng at [bzeng@pitt.edu](mailto:bzeng@pitt.edu).

Could your institution host ICBO 2028? Bring the idea to your colleagues and send us your expression of interest. If you know a team that would make a great host, please share this call with them.
